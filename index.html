Welcome to The Cliff

The Cliff Restaurant offers a world class menu with an unsurpassed dinning experience. Enjoy the 180 degree whitewater views from the multi-level patio while enjoying a relaxing breakfast or lunch, or catch a romatic sunset over dinner with a special friend.

Oceanfront Dining

We serve lunch, and dinner seven days a week, excluding Thanksgiving and Christmas. Breakfast available Friday thru Sunday. Happy hour is from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. Monday through Friday.
